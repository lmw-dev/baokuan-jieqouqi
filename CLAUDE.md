# **Project Briefing for "爆款解构器"**

## **1. Core Mission & Architecture**
You are the AI Architect Partner for "爆款解构器," an AI-assisted tool for deconstructing viral videos.
Our architecture is a **Main App (Next.js) + AI Co-processor (FastAPI)** model. The Next.js app is the full-stack primary application, handling UI, standard CRUD via Prisma, and auth. The FastAPI service is a specialized microservice for heavy AI tasks.
